What the tool does today

  • Write down a metric with any signature (p, q) on a finite-dimensional space, and check it in Lean.
  • Compute curvature, Ricci, and scalar curvature from coordinate components, and check the values in Lean.
  • Show Minkowski space is flat: Riemann, Ricci, and scalar all zero, checked in Lean.
  • Show the 2-sphere numbers at one point: Ricci equals the metric, scalar equals 2, checked in Lean.

What it does not do yet

  • Derive a connection from a metric on a curved manifold. The sphere symbols are hardcoded.
  • State or check the Einstein equations on curved space.
  • State the Kerr metric, or check Ric = 0 for Kerr.
  • Offer Sobolev spaces, embeddings, or energy estimates. Nothing in Phase 3 exists.
