What the tool does today

  • Write down a metric with any signature (p, q) on a finite-dimensional space, and check it in Lean.
  • Compute curvature, Ricci, and scalar curvature from coordinate components, and check the values in Lean.
  • Show Minkowski space is flat: Riemann, Ricci, and scalar all zero, checked in Lean.
  • Show the 2-sphere numbers: Ricci equals the metric, scalar equals 2, with the symbols derived from the metric, checked in Lean.
  • State the Einstein vacuum equations in both forms, and prove Schwarzschild Ric = 0 on its regular chart, checked in Lean.
  • Use W^{1,2} on R^n with weak derivatives, norm, and Hilbert structure, checked in Lean.

What it does not do yet

  • Derive a connection from a metric on a general curved manifold. The flat connection is the only one that exists.
  • State or check Ric = 0 for Kerr.
  • Offer Sobolev spaces on manifolds, embeddings, trace, or energy estimates. The first slice and the carrier design exist; the proofs are running.
  • Prove the bridge H^1 = W^{1,2}. It is stated forward-only.
